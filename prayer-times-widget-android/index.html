Available layouts and themes

Prayer widget layouts

Choose the amount of prayer information that suits your home screen. Every layout is available in light and dark themes.

Prayer information without opening the app

A prayer times widget lowers friction. Instead of opening an app and searching through a list, you can glance at your Android home screen for the next salah, its countdown, or the complete daily schedule.

Quick access to the Qibla compass

The green compass button opens Waqto Salat’s Qibla direction screen directly from the widget. It keeps the compass close without adding extra information to the prayer-time layout.

How to add a prayer time widget on Android

  1. 1

    Touch and hold an empty area of your Android home screen.

  2. 2

    Tap Widgets. The label may vary slightly by phone or launcher.

  3. 3

    Find Waqto Salat and browse the available layouts.

  4. 4

    Press and hold your preferred widget, then drag it onto the home screen.

  5. 5

    Move or resize it if your Android launcher supports resizing.

Designed for repeated checks

Prayer times are checked repeatedly throughout the day. Waqto Salat keeps widgets connected to a broader calm experience with accurate prayer time calculation, dynamic salah reminders, Qibla direction, multilingual support, and an ad-free interface.

Who should use it

The widget feature is useful if you want the next prayer to stay visible while working, studying, commuting, or planning your day around salah.

Why widgets help consistency

Prayer times are not checked once a day. They shape the rhythm of the whole day. A widget keeps that rhythm visible without opening another screen, searching for the next prayer, or waiting for a notification. In Waqto Salat, widgets work together with dynamic salah reminders, Qibla direction, and calibration so the home screen supports the same calm prayer flow as the app itself.

Prayer widget questions

Does Waqto Salat include Android prayer time widgets?

Yes. You can choose from a full daily schedule, a next-prayer strip, a detailed prayer view, and a compact countdown.

How do I add a prayer time widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty home-screen area, open Widgets, find Waqto Salat, then drag your preferred layout into place. Exact labels vary by launcher.

Can I use light and dark prayer widget themes?

Yes. The widgets are available in light and dark themes to suit different home screens.

What does the green compass button do?

It opens Waqto Salat’s Qibla direction screen directly from the prayer widget.

Do prayer time widgets replace salah reminders?

No. Widgets support quick checks, while dynamic reminders can notify you before or after a selected prayer time.
